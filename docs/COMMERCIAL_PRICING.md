# Commercial pricing proposal

Version 0.1 · 26/09/2026 · UK launch hypothesis

## Positioning

The Dog Club is operating software for a membership-led dog venue, rather than a low-cost appointment diary. The commercial value comes from joining member onboarding, dog and care records, grooming bookings, visit operations, payments, hospitality workflows, staffing and a branded member experience in one tenant-isolated product.

Prices below are proposed UK business-to-business prices excluding VAT. They are not an approved customer quotation or a forecast. Validate willingness to pay, onboarding effort and support cost with the first three design partners before fixing the public tariff.

## Recommended standard offer

| Item | Proposed price | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Platform licence | **£349 per location/month** | One operating location on the shared multi-tenant production platform |
| Initial onboarding | **£2,500 once** | Brand configuration, service and membership setup, one agreed data import, two remote training sessions and launch support |
| Additional location | **£149/month** | An additional location for the same operating business, subject to shared commercial ownership and support terms |
| Dedicated branded iOS and Android apps | **£1,500 setup + £149/month** | Managed builds and releases from the shared mobile codebase; store accounts and third-party charges excluded |
| Dedicated database/infrastructure | **From £5,000 setup + £1,250/month** | Contracted enterprise exception with separate credentials, backups, monitoring and operational responsibility |
| Bespoke product work | **£750–£1,000/day** | Agreed work outside supported configuration and the shared roadmap |
| SMS, WhatsApp and specialist connectors | **Usage or quoted add-on** | Provider charges, support load and capability determine the price |

The base platform licence should include member and household accounts, private dog/care records, optional public dog profiles, membership plans and paywall, grooming applications and approval, bookings and visit lifecycle, staff access and basic rotas, supported payments/refunds, operator reporting, hosting, backups and normal support. Only advertise a capability as included after its production acceptance gate passes.

## Founding Partner offer

Offer the first suitable design partner:

- **£1,500 onboarding**;
- **£249 per month for the first 12 months**; and
- migration to the prevailing standard platform price at renewal, stated in the signed order form.

In return, require structured product feedback, reasonable access for workflow validation and permission to request a case study. Any right to use the operator's name, premises, brand assets or customer material must be separately agreed. The discount does not include unlimited bespoke development or a promise that deferred integrations will be delivered by a fixed date.

## Commercial rules

1. Use a 12-month business agreement after any separately defined paid pilot, billed monthly by Direct Debit or card.
2. Charge the platform fee per location and avoid per-member pricing at launch. Review this when actual hosting, media, messaging and support costs are known.
3. Do not add a Dog Club percentage fee to membership, grooming or café receipts at launch. Each operator receives customer funds through its own supported merchant account and pays processor fees directly.
4. Keep processor charges, SMS/WhatsApp usage, POS hardware, payroll-provider fees, app-store accounts and unusual migration work outside the platform licence.
5. Define fair-use limits and prices before automated messaging, storage-heavy media or high-touch support goes live.
6. Treat separately branded store apps as a managed add-on. Store approval is not guaranteed and the operator should own the relevant developer accounts where platform rules require it.
7. Quote a dedicated database only for a contractual, regulatory, data-residency or measured scale need. It is not the default white-label deployment.
8. Put scope, response times, training, import limits, renewal price, exit/export and bespoke-work rates in every order form.

## Sales framing

At £349 per month, the platform costs approximately £11.50 per day. It is equivalent to the turnover from roughly six £59 memberships or seven £50 grooming appointments per month. This is a value illustration, not a claim about margin or guaranteed additional revenue.

Sell the operational result: fewer manual hand-offs, one view of each dog, easier recurring membership, safer grooming approval, bookable capacity, clearer collections and a branded experience members use. Do not sell unimplemented integrations or imply that software alone creates those additional bookings.

## Validation before public pricing

- Interview at least three prospective UK operators and test £249, £349 and £499 monthly anchors without leading them.
- Record expected member count, monthly bookings, locations, current software spend and hours lost to manual work.
- Measure onboarding hours and support contacts for the first live operator.
- Build a per-tenant cost report covering hosting, storage, media delivery, messages, payment support and mobile releases.
- Review price after the first three paid operators and again before the tenth; preserve signed founding terms rather than changing them informally.
- Have the order form, subscription terms, data-processing terms and VAT treatment reviewed before taking the first production customer payment.

## Initial decision

Use **£349 per location/month plus £2,500 onboarding** as the standard sales anchor. Use the Founding Partner offer only for a limited early cohort. Keep the product on one clear core price until real customer and support evidence justifies packages.
